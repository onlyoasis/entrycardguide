The public FAQ and guide expose the steps, documents, upload size and MRZ name rule. The personal-information form needs an activated account and exposes no readable regexes, character limits or official validation errors. This page invents none and mounts no local validator.

## Section 1: Route and eligibility

**1. Visa type.** Choose Tourist for a holiday. Business and religious visits use different documents or routes. The visa type cannot be changed after payment.

**2. Nationality eligibility.** Check the current Tourist list, not the Business list. The FAQ lists 100 Tourist nationalities and 54 Business nationalities.

**3. Passport validity.** Confirm at least 6 months remain. The issued eVisa is tied to the passport in the application.

## Section 2: Identity and uploads

**4. Full name.** Copy the full name from the two MRZ lines at the bottom of the passport bio page. Use English characters only.

**5. Passport number.** Use the passport you will carry. Review the number before payment because paid applications cannot be corrected.

**6. Face photo.** Upload a recent digital passport-sized photograph.

**7. Passport copy.** Upload a readable scan of the bio-data page. The guide accepts JPG or PDF up to 2 MB for each upload.

## Section 3: Tourist trip

**8. Hotel booking.** Upload the booking for the stay. Tourist and Business applications have different document lists.

**9. Return ticket.** Provide evidence showing how you leave Myanmar. For Kawthaung entry, the FAQ accepts a boat ticket and trip plan.

**10. Port of entry.** Choose Yangon, Mandalay or Nay Pyi Taw International Airport, or Kawthaung land checkpoint.

**11. Email and final review.** Use an inbox you control for activation, acknowledgement and the Approval Letter. Check the name, nationality, passport number, email and visa type before paying.

## Children and submission

A child under 7 included in the parent's passport goes in Child Info at Step 2. A child with a separate passport needs a separate paid application.

Normal processing starts after successful submission and payment and takes at least 3 working days. When approved, download and print one Approval Letter.
