## Fast facts

- **Official portal:** `evisa.moip.gov.mm/home`.
- **Agency:** Ministry of Immigration and Population.
- **Fee:** USD 50 in the official guide; verify the live Step 5 amount.
- **Processing:** minimum 3 working days after successful submission and payment.
- **Stay:** 28 days; Tourist eVisa cannot be extended.
- **Approval Letter:** valid 90 days from issue; print one copy.
- **Entry:** Yangon, Mandalay or Nay Pyi Taw International Airport, or Kawthaung land checkpoint.
