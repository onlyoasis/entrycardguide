## Check the passport before paying

The current VFS e-Visa application is available only to holders of valid Australian, Canadian and United States passports. Residence in one of those countries is not enough.

If the passport is exempt under Brazil's current rules, do not apply or pay. If the passport is from another country or eligibility is uncertain, use the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or a Brazilian consulate to identify the correct route.

## Verify the designated contractor

`brazil.vfsevisa.com` is a commercial domain, not a government suffix. VFS Global is the government-designated contractor that receives the online application. Brazilian immigration authorities decide it.

Start on `gov.br`. Confirm that the Ministry of Foreign Affairs page links to the same full VFS host, then continue.

## Fee, processing and validity

The published fee is **USD 80.90**. VFS says processing takes up to 10 business days after a verified application is submitted. A correction request delays that starting point, so apply early.

The live contractor page displays 5-year and 10-year multiple-entry validity branches for eligible passports. The government says the e-Visa has the same validity as the corresponding conventional visa and permits multiple entries. Read the exact validity on the issued PDF. The contractor publishes a stay limit of up to 90 days per year.

## Approval and travel

The approved e-Visa arrives by email. Check the passport details, save the PDF and print it. Travel with the same valid passport used in the application.
