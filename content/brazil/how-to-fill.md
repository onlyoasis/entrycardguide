Start from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs page. It links to `brazil.vfsevisa.com`, the government-designated commercial contractor. The `.com` host is not a government suffix.

{{< official-link site="brazil.government_authorization" >}}

{{< official-link site="brazil.evisa" >}}

The dynamic application does not publish stable regexes, character limits or validation messages. Follow the live controls rather than an old screenshot.

## Eligibility

Confirm that the applicant holds a valid Australian, Canadian or United States passport. If not, stop and check the current Brazilian government route instead of paying VFS.

## Passport and photograph

Upload a clear colour copy of the passport biographical page and a recent front-facing photograph. Use the same passport for travel. Follow the live file, background and pose instructions.

## Travel details and minors

Enter current trip details and provide additional evidence if requested. For a child, open the current minor-document list and prepare the requested parental consent and parent or guardian identity evidence.

## Email, payment and PDF

Use an applicant-controlled email, review the USD 80.90 payment and submit. Processing begins after the application is verified and submitted. Save the approved PDF from email, check every passport detail and print it for travel.
