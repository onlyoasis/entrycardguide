Do not start with payment. Brazil's current VFS e-Visa route is exclusively for eligible holders of valid Australian, Canadian and United States passports. Other travellers must check the current Brazilian government visa rules for their own passport.

The application host `brazil.vfsevisa.com` is operated by the government-designated commercial contractor VFS Global. Its `.com` domain is **not** a government suffix. Verify it by opening the Ministry of Foreign Affairs page on `gov.br` and following that page's e-Visa link.

## Fast facts

- **Government verification:** start at `gov.br`, Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
- **Designated application host:** `brazil.vfsevisa.com`, operated by VFS Global.
- **Domain warning:** `.com` is not a Brazilian government suffix.
- **Eligibility:** current route for Australian, Canadian and U.S. passports.
- **Fee:** USD 80.90.
- **Processing:** up to 10 business days after verified submission.
- **Result:** approved e-Visa PDF delivered by email; print and carry it with the matching passport.
