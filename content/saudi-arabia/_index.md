Saudi Arabia's tourist eVisa is open to **66 eligible-country passports**. It is normally valid for one year, allows multiple entries, and permits stays of up to 90 days.

Start from the government page above, then follow it out to the application. The tourist eVisa portal is operated by the Ministry of Tourism, but its hostname does not end in `.gov.sa`, so reaching it from a government page and checking the address bar is part of the process.

## Fast facts

- **Application:** the tourist eVisa portal operated by the Saudi Ministry of Tourism. Its host is not a `.gov.sa` suffix.
- **Government starting point:** `my.gov.sa`.
- **Eligibility:** 66 eligible-country passports.
- **Validity:** normally one year, multiple entry, up to 90 days per stay.
- **Passport:** at least 6 months of validity at entry.
- **Fee:** SAR 300 base government fee. Mandatory medical insurance, VAT and the payment amount affect the checkout total.
- **Covers:** tourism, events, family and relative visits, leisure, and Umrah excluding Hajj.
- **Does not cover:** Hajj, study or work.
