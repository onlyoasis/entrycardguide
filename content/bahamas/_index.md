The Bahamas has a government-authorized digital Customs C17 route at `exempt.gov.bs`. Check the traveller category before opening it.

{{< official-link site="bahamas.government_entry" >}}

Every returning resident completes C17. A visitor uses C17 when bringing items beyond used personal belongings. A visitor with only used personal belongings makes an oral baggage declaration under the current government page.

## Fast facts

- **Digital C17 portal:** `exempt.gov.bs`.
- **Government authority:** Bahamas Customs Department and Ministry of Finance.
- **Developer/operator:** Exempt Technologies.
- **Filing service:** Free; customs duties are separate.
- **Output:** QR code for the designated Exempt customs lane.
- **Separate 2026 development:** the Bahamas Digital Arrival Card is still a pilot for select visitors on selected flights, with paper remaining in parallel.
