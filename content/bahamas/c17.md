## Check whether C17 is your route

{{< official-link site="bahamas.government_entry" >}}

The current government entry page gives three routes:

- **Returning resident:** complete C17 whether or not there are items to declare.
- **Visitor bringing other items:** complete a C17 baggage declaration and declare them.
- **Visitor with only used personal belongings:** make an oral baggage declaration.

This is narrower than the Exempt landing-page sentence that says everyone fills C17. Use the government traveller-specific rule instead of sending every visitor to an unnecessary form.

## Official digital C17 portal and operator

{{< official-link site="bahamas.c17" >}}

`exempt.gov.bs` says it is officially authorized by Bahamas Customs and the Ministry of Finance. The same page says the app was developed by Exempt Technologies. This is a government-suffix portal with a named private developer, not an unidentified lookalike.

The portal asks the user to scan passport information into a profile before submitting forms. After C17 submission, it issues a QR code for the designated Exempt lane, where Customs reviews the electronic declaration.

## Free form, separate customs duty

{{< official-link site="bahamas.operator_terms" >}}

Exempt Technologies says the service is provided at no cost. The government-authorized portal can calculate and prepay customs duties. A duty charged on declared goods is not a fee for opening or submitting C17.

## Do not confuse C17 with the BDAC pilot

{{< official-link site="bahamas.digital_arrival_pilot" >}}

On 5 May 2026, the Ministry of Tourism announced a soft pilot for a Bahamas Digital Arrival Card that combines immigration and customs. The release limits it to select visitors on selected flights and says the traditional paper process remains alongside it.

Do not search for or pay a site claiming every visitor must buy a BDAC. Follow instructions supplied for the selected flight. For the established customs route, use the C17 decision above.

## What the digital C17 covers

The public C17 page is titled “BAHAMAS DIGITAL ENTRY CARD FORM NO. C17.” It groups the declaration into Passenger, Travel, Baggage, Invoice, Alcohol, Tobacco and Submission information, and says all fields are mandatory.
