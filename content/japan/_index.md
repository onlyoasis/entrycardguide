## Who has to file it

- **Customs declaration:** Japan Customs requires every passenger entering Japan to submit a declaration of accompanied and unaccompanied articles.
- **Visit Japan Web:** Customs recommends doing that declaration electronically in Visit Japan Web. The paper Customs Declaration Form can still be used.
- **By nationality:** there is no US, UK or Australian version. Every passenger makes the same customs declaration, online or on paper.

Source: {{< source-link site="japan.customs_declaration" text="Japan Customs declaration page" >}}. Whether you also need a visa is a separate question.

## The fast facts

- **Official source:** Digital Agency service page under `.go.jp`.
- **Cost:** free.
- **What it is for:** arrival, customs, and related QR workflows.
- **What it is not:** a visa approval or immigration status guarantee.
