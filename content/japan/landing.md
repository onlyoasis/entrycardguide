This page covers what happens after you land at Narita (NRT) or Haneda (HND): the checks in order, the official limits at customs, how to get into Tokyo, and what changes for tax-free shopping on 1 November 2026. Every limit links to the government or airport page it comes from.

Visit Japan Web is covered in [the Visit Japan Web guide](/japan/visit-japan-web/). It is free.

## The order after you land

Japan Customs lists the customs inspection area as the last stop, after these ([source](https://www.customs.go.jp/kaigairyoko/nyuukoku.htm), checked 2026-09-12):

1. **Quarantine.**
2. **Immigration.** Passport, plus the Visit Japan Web immigration QR if you made one.
3. **Baggage claim, and animal and plant quarantine** if you carry meat, fruit or plants.
4. **Customs.** Visit Japan Web customs QR, or the paper declaration form.

## Immigration

Foreign visitors arriving on a short stay go through the staffed booths. The Immigration Services Agency describes its face-recognition gates for foreign nationals as a departure procedure only ([source](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/immigration/resources/nyuukokukanri07_00168.html?lang=en), checked 2026-10-14). The departure side is covered below.

## Customs: what you can bring without duty

Everyone entering Japan submits the declaration of accompanied and unaccompanied goods, through Visit Japan Web or on paper. A family examined together can hand in one paper form through one person. Limits per adult, from Japan Customs ([source](https://www.customs.go.jp/kaigairyoko/menzei.htm), checked 2026-09-12):

| Item | Duty-free limit |
|---|---|
| Alcohol | 3 bottles, 760 ml each |
| Cigarettes | 200. Heated tobacco, cigars and other tobacco follow separate quantities on the Customs table |
| Perfume | 2 oz, about 56 ml. Eau de cologne and eau de toilette are not counted |
| Other goods | Total overseas value up to JPY 200,000 |

- Under-20s get no alcohol or tobacco allowance. Children under 6 get it only for items that are clearly their own, such as toys.
- Customs picks which items fit inside the JPY 200,000 and taxes the rest. A single item over JPY 200,000 is taxed in full.
- A category totalling JPY 10,000 or less is generally exempt.
- Goods you ship separately count toward the same allowance if they arrive within six months.

## Cash: when you must declare

From Japan Customs ([source](https://www.customs.go.jp/kaigairyoko/shiharaishudan.htm), checked 2026-09-12):

- Declare if your **cash, cheques (including traveller's cheques), promissory notes and securities together exceed the equivalent of JPY 1,000,000**. Japanese and foreign currency count together.
- Separately, declare gold bullion of 90% purity or more that weighs over 1 kg.
- The rule applies **on arrival and on departure**. The form is the declaration of carrying means of payment, available at customs or printable from the Customs site in Japanese and English.

## Getting into Tokyo

**From Narita.** Narita Airport's rail page lists these lines ([source](https://www.narita-airport.jp/en/access/train/), checked 2026-10-14):

- **Narita Express** (JR East): Tokyo Station, Shinagawa, Shibuya, Shinjuku, Yokohama.
- **Keisei Skyliner**: Nippori and Keisei Ueno, for Ueno, Asakusa and Ikebukuro.
- **Keisei Access Express** and **Keisei Main Line**: toward Nippori and Keisei Ueno with more stops. The page also lists the Toei Asakusa Line (Asakusa, Shinbashi, Sengakuji).
- **JR Line**: toward Tokyo Station, Shinagawa, Ueno and other JR stations.

Fares and timetables are on each operator's site; the airport page links to them.

**From Haneda.** The airport is directly connected to the **Keikyu Line** and the **Tokyo Monorail** ([source](https://tokyo-haneda.com/en/access/train/index.html), checked 2026-10-14). Haneda Airport's own estimates: Shinagawa about 15 minutes, Hamamatsucho about 20, Tokyo Station about 30, Shibuya about 45, Shinjuku about 50. Fares are on each company's site.

**Taxis at Haneda** have two kinds of stands: general stands, and stands for app-dispatched or reserved taxis ([source](https://tokyo-haneda.com/en/access/index.html), checked 2026-10-14). If you booked through an app, go to the second kind.

## Getting online

If your phone supports eSIM, you can buy and install a data plan before you fly and switch it on after landing. Check the phone's settings first: many phones sold in mainland China do not support eSIM.

## Tax-free shopping from 1 November 2026

From the Japan Tourism Agency ([source](https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/tax-free/page01_000001_00028.html), checked 2026-10-14):

- Purchases up to 31 October 2026 follow the current system. Purchases from **1 November 2026** use the refund method.
- At the shop you pay the **tax-inclusive price**. The consumption tax is refunded later, by the shop or by a refund service company it uses. Ask the shop how it pays.
- On departure, present your passport at a tax-free procedure terminal or kiosk in the international departure lobby. The terminal tells you whether Customs needs to inspect the goods. **Do this before you check in your bags**; it cannot be done afterwards.
- The deadline is **90 days from the date of purchase**.
- Purchases must total at least JPY 5,000, excluding tax, per store per day.
- Keep every item from a receipt together. If one item from a purchase is missing at inspection, none of the goods on that receipt qualify.

## When you leave

- The cash rule above applies again.
- Foreign nationals on a short-stay status can use the **face-recognition gates** at departure without registering, if they hold an IC passport, can operate the gate alone, and are at least 135 cm tall. Departures on a re-entry permit are excluded ([source](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/immigration/resources/nyuukokukanri07_00168.html?lang=en), checked 2026-10-14).
- The gate does not stamp your passport. If you want a stamp, ask the staff behind the gate or at the booth before boarding.
