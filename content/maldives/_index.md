Every foreign arrival in the Maldives submits the IMUGA Traveller Declaration within the **96 hours before arrival**. It is free, and it runs on a government host.

{{< official-link site="maldives.traveller_declaration" >}}

Outbound travellers no longer submit anything. Maldives Immigration removed the departure declaration from **15 August 2024**. Guides that still tell you to file one before flying home are out of date.

## Who has to file it

- **Must file:** all travellers, within 96 hours before their arrival flight. Each traveller files their own declaration, children and minors included.
- **Does not file:** Maldivian citizens. Immigration discontinued the Traveller Declaration for Maldivians.
- **By nationality:** there is no Indian, US or Chinese version. Every foreign passport uses the same free IMUGA form.

Whether you also need a visa is a separate question.

## Fast facts

- **Official portal:** `imuga.immigration.gov.mv`, under the government `.gov.mv` domain.
- **Agency:** Maldives Immigration.
- **Cost:** free. There is no official express tier and no official service charge.
- **Who submits:** all foreign arrivals.
- **When:** within the 96 hours before arrival.
- **Departure:** no longer required since 15 August 2024.
- **Visa:** the tourist visa is issued on arrival with no pre-approval. The declaration does not replace it.
