## What the declaration is

{{< official-link site="maldives.traveller_declaration" >}}

The IMUGA Traveller Declaration is a free submission that every foreign arrival makes within the **96 hours before arrival**. The portal sits under `immigration.gov.mv`, the Maldives government domain.

It is one submission per arrival. Nothing in the official flow charges a fee, offers priority handling, or asks you to buy anything.

## Departure declarations ended in 2024

{{< official-link site="maldives.departure_change" >}}

Maldives Immigration announced on **13 August 2024** that outbound travellers are no longer required to submit the declaration, effective **15 August 2024**.

If a page or a message tells you to file a departure form before flying home, it is describing a rule that was removed two years ago. There is nothing to pay for and nothing to file.

## The declaration is not the visa

{{< official-link site="maldives.tourist_visa" >}}

The tourist visa is issued **on arrival**, and there is no pre-approval step to buy in advance. Anyone selling a Maldives tourist visa approval before travel is selling something the official process does not have.

The Traveller Declaration is the separate part, and it is still mandatory for foreign arrivals. The two do not replace each other.

## What the form collects

{{< official-link site="maldives.declaration_info" >}}

The public first step of the portal reads the **passport data page** by scan or upload, asks the **purpose of visit**, and asks for a **permit number and expiry** when a permit applies to that traveller.

The official tourist entry page also names **personal details**, **travel information** and **accommodation details** among the requirements.

That is what the public pages confirm. This guide does not list field lengths or error messages for the live form, because the portal does not publish them.
