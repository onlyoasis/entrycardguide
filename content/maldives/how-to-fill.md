The IMUGA portal does not publish regexes, character limits or error text for the live form, so none appear here. This guide covers the items the public first step and the official tourist entry page confirm.

## Before you start: timing

Submit within the **96 hours before arrival**. Submitting earlier than the window opens is not the goal; being finished before you reach the airport is.

Departure is not part of this. Since 15 August 2024, outbound travellers submit nothing.

## 1. Passport data page

The first public step scans or uploads the passport data page. Have the passport itself, or a clear image of the data page, ready before you open the portal. Photograph the whole page in even light rather than cropping to the printed number.

## 2. Personal details

{{< official-link site="maldives.tourist_visa" >}}

Personal details are named on the official tourist entry page. Copy them from the passport data page. A name shortened or reordered by an airline booking system is not the reference.

## 3. Purpose of visit

The public first step asks for the purpose of the visit. Answer it as it is. The tourist visa issued at the counter covers a short visit, not employment or study, so a purpose chosen to look convenient can create a problem it was meant to avoid.

## 4 and 5. Permit number and expiry, when they apply

These two are conditional. They appear only for travellers a permit applies to, and they come from the permit document itself. If the portal does not ask you for them, there is nothing to fill in and nothing missing.

## 6. Travel information

Use the itinerary for the arrival this declaration covers. One declaration goes with one arrival, submitted inside the 96-hour window.

## 7. Accommodation details

Give the resort, guesthouse or hotel for the stay. The official page asks for accommodation details, so an island name on its own is thinner than what is being requested.

## After you submit

{{< official-link site="maldives.declaration_info" >}}

Keep whatever confirmation the portal returns available on arrival. The declaration is free, so a payment screen has no place in this flow. The tourist visa is still issued at the counter, and the declaration does not decide admission.
