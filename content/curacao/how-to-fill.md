The portal publishes its information groups and several field rules, but not live regexes, maxlengths or validation messages. Follow the current controls.

## Travel information

Open the form within seven days before departure. Use the current arrival date and operating flight number. The flight lookup should show the active carrier and port of embarkation.

## Passport information

Use the same passport carried to Curaçao. Uploading a passport image is optional. If it fails, complete the required fields manually. Copy the name shown in the passport and do not use a nickname.

## Personal and contact information

Match the passport date of birth. Use an email you can open at airline check-in. The PDF is emailed there; parents or guardians can use their own address for a child.

## Review, submit and save

Accept the disclaimer only after reading it, review the card and submit. Download the PDF immediately and keep the email copy.

If you find a mistake, use “Yes, edit existing Immigration Card” instead of creating another active card. The official FAQ says one application per person per trip. Show the saved confirmation at airline check-in and immigration.
