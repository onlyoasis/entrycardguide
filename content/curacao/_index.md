All foreign visitors to Curaçao must complete a Digital Immigration Card for each person and each trip. Residents are exempt. The card is free and separate from visa status.

The application host is `dicardcuracao.com`, not a government suffix. Verify it by starting from the Curaçao Tourist Board entry page, which links directly to that portal and says the DI Card costs nothing.

## Fast facts

- **Designated portal:** `dicardcuracao.com/dicard/`.
- **Verification source:** Curaçao Tourist Board at `curacao.com`.
- **Cost:** free. No payment is required.
- **When:** within seven days before departure; the form can still be filed up to one hour before arrival.
- **Who:** each foreign visitor, including each child, per trip. Curaçao residents do not file.
- **Result:** downloadable or emailed PDF shown at airline check-in and immigration.
