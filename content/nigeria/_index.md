Nigeria Immigration Service (NIS) runs the free Landing & Exit Card portal, launched on 1 May 2025. Choose the relevant arrival or departure service; an arrival card does not automatically cover leaving Nigeria. The card does not replace a required visa.

## Before you travel

- **Who:** the dedicated portal FAQ addresses foreign travellers. NIS's general introduction says all passengers; Nigerian citizens, dual nationals and special-status travellers should confirm their case with NIS.
- **Transit:** the portal excludes pure transit. A connection requiring entry is not automatically exempt.
- **When:** complete the card before boarding and before the relevant border inspection. This guide does not state a fixed advance-hour window.
- **Cost:** the card is free; visa and other travel fees are separate.
- **What to keep:** download and print the card or exit confirmation, and retain the email.
