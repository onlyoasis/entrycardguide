The live application publishes no regexes, character limits or error text, so this guide states none. What follows is what the government portal and its FAQ do publish, split into the three stages where mistakes actually cost money.

## Section 1: Eligibility and visa type

**1. Check the eligible-nationality list.**

{{< official-link site="egypt.faq" >}}

The FAQ lists the nationalities that can currently use the eVisa. Read it before opening an application, not after paying.

**2. Choose single or multiple entry.** Tourism fees are USD 30 for single entry and USD 65 for multiple entry. Two singles cost more than one multiple, so pick from the trip rather than from the smaller number.

## Section 2: The application

**3. Passport number.** Copy it from the passport you will travel on. The issued eVisa is tied to that document, so renewing the passport means applying again.

**4. Passport validity.** The entry checklist requires at least 6 months from arrival. Check the expiry before paying; the fee does not come back.

**5. Travel dates and lead time.** The portal says to create the application at least 7 days before departure. Seven days is the floor.

**6. Email.** Approval and the download link arrive here. Use an address you control, because retrieving your own eVisa from someone else's inbox depends on them.

A payment confirmation is not an approval. Wait for the approval message before treating the visa as issued.

## Section 3: The entry checklist

**7. Printed eVisa.** The checklist names a printed copy. Print it before leaving home.

**8. Itinerary.** Keep the trip plan available on arrival.

**9. Hotel booking or accommodation details.** Named for tourism visits. Save it so it opens without wifi.

**10. Supporting letter.** Named for business or family visits. A plain holiday does not need one.

## One more thing about visas on arrival

{{< official-link site="egypt.embassy_visa_requirements" >}}

That host is the Egyptian Embassy in Washington's own site rather than a government suffix. It states that travellers on the pre-approval list cannot obtain a visa on arrival, that Bangladeshi nationals require a visa before entry, and that airlines should confirm boarding eligibility.

For passports on the portal's list, the eVisa is the route you can plan around. Arrival visas depend on the passport and the port.
