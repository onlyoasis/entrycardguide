Egypt's e-Visa is applied for on a government portal run by the Ministry of Interior. Current tourism fees are **USD 30 single entry** and **USD 65 multiple entry**.

{{< official-link site="egypt.evisa" >}}

The portal is not open to every passport. The official FAQ lists the nationalities that can currently use the eVisa, so that list is the first thing to read.

## Fast facts

- **Official portal:** `visa2egypt.gov.eg`, under the government `.gov.eg` domain.
- **Agency:** Ministry of Interior, Arab Republic of Egypt.
- **Fees:** USD 30 single-entry tourism, USD 65 multiple-entry tourism.
- **Lead time:** create the application at least 7 days before departure.
- **Eligibility:** the FAQ lists the nationalities that can use the eVisa.
- **Result:** approval and the download link arrive by email.
- **At the border:** passport valid at least 6 months from arrival, printed eVisa, itinerary, hotel details for tourism, and a supporting letter for a business or family visit.
