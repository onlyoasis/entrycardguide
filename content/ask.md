## What this tool is

A question box over this site's own verified content: the 250+ FAQ answers from the country guides, plus the official-URL, fee and verification-date data behind every country card. When you ask, the engine matches your question against that index in your browser and returns the closest verified answer with its source page. Name a country and it also pins the official-form card: government URL, fee, agency, last verified.

It answers in English and Chinese, and it never invents. If nothing verified matches, it says so and points you at the decide tool or the directory instead of guessing.

## What it is not

Not a chatbot and not a government service. There is no AI generating prose on the fly, no account, no log of what you asked. The index downloads once from this site; after that, everything runs locally.

## When to skip it and read instead

- You already know the country — open its guide directly from the Countries menu.
- You want every field of a form explained in order — the how-to-fill page for each country does that.
- You are not sure which form applies — the decide tool answers that in 60 seconds.
