## Prepare the details DGM asks for {#prepare}

The E-Ticket service covers entry and exit. DGM lists your full name, passport number, email, flight number, airline, exact accommodation address and applicable customs information among its requirements. The government service is free.

Keep your passport and booking confirmation beside you. Check that the direction of travel and dates match the journey you are completing. Use the address supplied by your accommodation provider.

The field cards below are a local aid, not a reproduction of every screen in the live form. Follow the official service if its questions differ.

## Save the result {#confirmation}

Save the confirmation and any QR code the service generates. Keep the result available offline for your journey. Complete the required entry and exit information; do not assume that one code automatically covers both directions.

If you cannot find a confirmation, use the official service's retrieval or help options before creating another submission. This guide cannot look up your record.
