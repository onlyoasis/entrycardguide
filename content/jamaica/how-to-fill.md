The public government HTML was read on 30 August 2026. It exposes required attributes, `minlength="6"` on the email-verification input, `maxlength="4"` on the flight-number box and an `mm/dd/yyyy` travel-date prompt. It publishes no passport regex or complete official error set, so none is invented here and no local validator is shown.

## 1. Verify an email you control

The form blocks progress until email verification is complete. Use an inbox you can open now and during the trip. The public input has a 6-character minimum. Government guidance says the confirmation email is sent to the address used.

## 2. Copy personal details from the passport

Enter last and first names from the passport. Middle name is not marked required in the live HTML. Choose the birth date and gender against the same document, then select the number of family members travelling with you.

## 3. Complete every address part

The home address is split into street, city or town, state or region, postal code and country. The Jamaica address asks for a hotel name or residential address. Use the first real place of stay, not just “Jamaica.”

## 4. Match the arriving leg

Select the carrier, then enter the flight-number portion in the box capped at 4 characters. Departure Port means the port immediately before arrival in Jamaica. Use the official picker for Travel Date; its prompt is `mm/dd/yyyy`.

## 5. State purpose and stay length

Choose the listed purpose that describes this trip. The length field asks for days. Read any travel-history prompt on the live form before selecting countries.

## 6. Separate the two luggage counts

“Accompanied” covers checked and hand luggage travelling with you. “Unaccompanied” covers luggage sent by air or sea freight. Do not merge the counts.

## 7. Read every customs question

The form asks about food, plants and animal products, restricted goods, currency or monetary instruments, commercial goods and items above the allowance. Its warning says failure to make a full declaration can lead to fines, forfeiture and imprisonment. If in doubt, declare the goods to the customs officer.

## 8. Submit within the government window

The government allows submission up to 30 days before travel. Check the passport, flight and customs answers before submitting, then keep the confirmation email accessible.
