## Use the current government portal

{{< official-link site="jamaica.c5" >}}

The current address is `enterjamaica.gov.jm`. Its title calls it the official Electronic Immigration and Customs Declaration Card, and its footer shows PICA and Jamaica Customs Agency.

Jamaica Customs says all arriving passengers must submit the Electronic Passenger Declaration for final clearance at international airports. The same notice says the service is free for visitors and residents.

## No contractor or paid filing service is needed

{{< official-link site="jamaica.government_warning" >}}

PICA issued a direct warning that no third-party entity or agent had been contracted to provide the online passenger declaration. That 2023 warning named the older `.com` address. Current Jamaica Customs and Ministry of Tourism pages point to `enterjamaica.gov.jm`.

Do not pay a site to “unlock,” “review” or “speed up” the C5. The government form itself is free.

## C5 is not a visa or Tourist Card

C5 combines passenger immigration information with a customs declaration. It does not decide whether a passport needs a visa, and it is not a paid Tourist Card product. Check any visa requirement separately for the passport. Do not treat a visa or card fee as a C5 fee.

## When to submit

{{< official-link site="jamaica.government_facts" >}}

The government facts page allows submission up to 30 days before travel. It asks travellers to use a valid email and relevant flight information, and says a confirmation email follows submission.

## What the public form asks

The live form covers:

- verified email;
- passport names, birth date, gender, family count, home address and occupation;
- passport number and issuing country;
- Jamaica address, travel history, carrier, flight number, departure port, arrival date, purpose and stay length;
- accompanied and unaccompanied luggage;
- customs questions about goods, currency and allowances.

→ [Prepare the Jamaica C5 fields](/jamaica/how-to-fill/)

## At arrival

Keep the confirmation email accessible. The C5 information is used for final immigration and customs clearance. A submitted declaration does not remove the duty to answer an officer truthfully or declare goods when in doubt.
