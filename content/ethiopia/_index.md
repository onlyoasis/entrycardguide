Ethiopia's Tourist eVisa is a government-issued, single-entry visa for tourism and other non-business travel. The current official prices are USD 62 for 30 days and USD 152 for 90 days.

{{< official-link site="ethiopia.evisa" >}}

Check the separate exemption and visa-on-arrival page before paying. Kenya and Djibouti citizens with valid passports are exempt.

## Fast facts

- **Official portal:** `evisa.gov.et`.
- **Agency:** Immigration and Citizenship Service of Ethiopia.
- **Visa:** Tourist Visa, single entry.
- **Fee:** USD 62 for 30 days; USD 152 for 90 days.
- **Processing:** normally 3 days; apply at least 3 days before intended arrival.
- **Validity:** starts from the intended entry date.
- **Documents:** recent passport photo and passport valid at least 6 months from intended entry.
