## Fast facts

- **Official portal:** `evisa.gov.bh`.
- **Operator:** Nationality, Passports & Residence Affairs (NPRA).
- **Routes:** online eVisa and visa on arrival have separate eligibility lists.
- **Published Visit eVisa totals:** BD 13, BD 29, BD 44 or BD 64, depending on the eligible product.
- **Published timing:** approximately 3 to 5 working days; exceptional cases can take longer.
- **Listed attachments:** passport copy, hotel reservation, return ticket and a 3-month bank statement in English.
- **Output:** decision by email, followed by retrieval and printing on the official portal.
- **Border document:** use the same passport entered in the application.
