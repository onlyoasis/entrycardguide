The public selection screen exposes route questions for GCC status, purpose, detailed purpose, description and passport nationality. Later form regexes, character limits and most error text were not publicly readable. This guide does not invent them.

## 1. Compare the two eligibility lists

{{< official-link site="bahrain.online_countries" >}}

{{< official-link site="bahrain.arrival_countries" >}}

Check the passport against both official lists. Terms and conditions apply to each. If the on-arrival route fits, do not assume an online application is also required.

## 2. Answer the GCC-status question

The live screen asks whether you hold a current valid residence permit or visit visa for a GCC country. Answer with the document's current status, then select the GCC country if the answer is yes.

## 3. Use the real purpose

Choose the purpose, detailed purpose and description that match the trip. Keep them consistent with the hotel, return booking and any guarantor information.

## 4. Select passport nationality

Use the nationality shown in the passport you will present at the border. NPRA says an approved traveller must arrive with the same passport used in the online application.

## 5. Choose only an eligible product

Do not pick only by price or headline duration. The eligibility result controls which products are available and provides their conditions. Current government pages describe two-week single-entry, three-month multiple-entry, one-year multiple-entry and five-year multiple-entry products.

## 6. Prepare the passport copy

Upload a clear copy of the passport you will use. Keep the identity details readable and do not switch travel documents after applying.

## 7. Prepare the hotel reservation

The covered visitor eVisa services list a hotel reservation. Check that the guest details and dates match the application.

## 8. Prepare the return ticket

The government service pages also list a return ticket. Use a booking with the same passenger and travel dates described in the application.

## 9. Prepare the financial evidence

The covered products ask for a 3-month bank statement in English. The published test is regular income of BD 300 to BD 500 per month or funds equivalent to BD 150 for each day of the visit.

## 10. Leave processing time

{{< official-link site="bahrain.faq" >}}

The published estimate is approximately 3 to 5 working days. Exceptional cases can take longer, so do not build a non-refundable trip around the shortest estimate.

## 11. Save the email and reference

NPRA emails the decision. Keep the reference number. If approved, retrieve and print the eVisa through the official portal, then travel with the same passport used for the application.
