The portal and official Form E.1 publish the information groups. The protected live application exposes no readable regexes, character limits or validation messages, so none are invented here.

## Travel information

Start after the form becomes available 72 hours before arrival. Use the operating flight or vessel and the confirmed accommodation address. Enter the planned length of this stay, not a visa validity period.

## Passenger identity

Use the passport carried to Barbados. Copy the last name, given names and date of birth from that document. The Immigration Department says the passport must remain valid for the duration of the trip.

## Customs and email

Answer the Customs section for everything carried in cabin and checked baggage. Use a valid email inbox available during travel.

## Submit and save both receipts

Review the Immigration and Customs answers before submission. Save both emailed receipts to the device and print backups if possible. Present the Immigration receipt and Customs receipt to their respective services on arrival.
