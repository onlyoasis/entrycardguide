Barbados combines immigration and customs questions in one online ED Form. The government portal opens 72 hours before arrival. It asks travellers to prepare flight information, passenger passports and a valid email address.

The official service is free. Do not pay a lookalike site for the standard ED Form. After submission, save both receipts: one for Immigration and one for Customs.

## Who has to file it

- **Must file:** travellers arriving in Barbados complete the online Immigration and Customs Form.
- **Exemptions:** we found no current official text on residents, flight crew or airport transit, so this page does not list any.
- **By nationality:** there is no Canadian, UK or German version. Every arriving traveller uses the same free form.

Source: {{< source-link site="barbados.visitor_rules" text="Barbados Immigration visitor page" >}}. Whether you also need a visa is a separate question.

## Fast facts

- **Official portal:** `travelform.gov.bb`.
- **Purpose:** combined Immigration and Customs Form.
- **Cost:** free.
- **When:** available 72 hours before arrival.
- **Prepare:** flight information, passenger passports and a valid email.
- **Result:** separate Immigration and Customs receipts, saved digitally or printed.
