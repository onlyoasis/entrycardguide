Check-Mig preloads trip information before Colombian immigration control. Migración Colombia says the service is free and optional. It is available to Colombian and foreign travellers entering or leaving the country.

Use the application on `apps.migracioncolombia.gov.co`. A paid Check-Mig seller is not collecting a Colombian government fee.

## Who has to file it

- **Nobody is required to:** Check-Mig is a free online pre-registration, and Migración Colombia says its use is not mandatory.
- **Who can use it:** Colombian nationals and foreigners entering or leaving Colombia.
- **By nationality:** there is no separate form by nationality. Pre-registering is optional for every passport.

Sources: {{< source-link site="colombia.service_guide" text="Migración Colombia service guide" >}} and {{< source-link site="colombia.checkmig" text="Check-Mig portal" >}}. Whether you also need a visa is a separate question.

## Fast facts

- **Official app:** `apps.migracioncolombia.gov.co/pre-registro/public/`.
- **Agency:** Unidad Administrativa Especial Migración Colombia.
- **Cost:** free.
- **Status:** optional and recommended to speed up immigration control.
- **When:** from 72 hours before travel until one hour before travel.
- **Direction:** select entry to Colombia or exit from Colombia for the current movement.
- **Result:** near-immediate email confirmation, with an official check-and-download option.
