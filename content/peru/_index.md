Peru replaced the paper movement card and passport stamp for international flight immigration control with an electronic record. The Virtual Andean Migration Card, or TAM Virtual, is generated automatically during immigration control.

You do not submit a TAM application before travel. After Migraciones records the movement, use the free official lookup to download proof of the entry or departure and review the authorized stay recorded there.

## Fast facts

- **Official lookup:** `cel.migraciones.gob.pe`.
- **Agency:** Superintendencia Nacional de Migraciones.
- **Cost:** free.
- **Creation:** automatic during immigration control.
- **Inputs:** document type and number, nationality, movement date and CAPTCHA.
- **Result:** downloadable TAM Virtual movement record.
