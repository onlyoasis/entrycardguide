## Generated at immigration control

The TAM Virtual is not a pre-arrival questionnaire. Migraciones creates the electronic document automatically during immigration control. It records the traveller's identity, movement date and time, checkpoint and authorized stay.

Peru also eliminated passport stamping for international flight immigration control. The electronic TAM is therefore the record to check and keep.

The lookup is free. It asks for the document used at the border, nationality, the arrival or departure date and a CAPTCHA.

## Check the authorized stay

Download the returned TAM and review the movement and authorized stay recorded by Migraciones. Do not calculate permission from an old blog or assume that a planned itinerary changed the official record.

If the record is missing or incorrect, contact Migraciones. Repeating the lookup does not amend the border record.
