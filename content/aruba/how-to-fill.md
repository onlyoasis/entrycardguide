The live official application publishes selected length and character controls. It does not publish its server-side error wording. Follow the current controls if they change.

## Travel information

Open the card within seven days before travel. Enter the confirmed arrival and departure dates. Use the operating arrival flight number, not a booking reference. The custom-flight control accepts letters and numbers and has a 10 character maximum.

## Passport information

Use the passport carried to Aruba. The live application normalizes the passport number to uppercase letters and numbers and exposes a 5 to 30 character range. First names and surname are required, each with a 50 character maximum. Copy them from the passport.

## Personal and contact information

Enter the current residence address; the visible address line has a 42 character maximum. Choose the purpose for this trip and use an email inbox available during travel.

## Review, pay if applicable and save

Review all fields. Most air arrivals then pay the USD 20 sustainability fee; exemptions and the sea-arrival rule remain available on the official fee page. Save the approved ED Card confirmation and keep both digital and printed copies.
