## Who has to file it

- **Must file:** arriving Filipino and foreign passengers; arriving Filipino and foreign crew; departing Filipino passengers.
- **Does not file:** foreign diplomats and their dependants; foreign dignitaries and their delegations; 9(e) visa holders; diplomatic and official or service passport holders.
- **By nationality:** there is no US, Tongan or Chinese version. Foreign tourists and returning Filipinos register on the same free eTravel site, and dual citizens are passengers like everyone else.

Source: {{< source-link site="philippines.faq" text="eTravel FAQ" >}}. Whether you also need a visa is a separate question.

## The fast facts

- **Official URL:** `etravel.gov.ph`.
- **Cost:** free.
- **What you need:** passport, flight or vessel details, arrival or departure date, email.
- **What you get:** a QR confirmation. Screenshot it.
- **What it is not:** a visa.
