ETA-IL is Israel's electronic travel authorisation for short visitors from visa-exempt countries. It became mandatory on 1 January 2025. It does not turn a visa-required passport into a visa-exempt one.

{{< official-link site="israel.eligibility" >}}

If the PIBA eligibility check says your passport needs a B/2 visitor visa, do not pay for ETA-IL. Follow the visa instruction from an Israeli mission instead. If the result says ETA-IL, apply only at the government portal.

## Fast facts

- **Official URL:** `israel-entry.piba.gov.il`.
- **Agency:** Population and Immigration Authority (PIBA).
- **Eligibility:** citizens of visa-exempt countries visiting for short tourism or business.
- **Cost:** ILS 25, non-refundable. Official notices say there is no express option.
- **Decision:** often immediate, but allow up to 72 hours.
- **Validity:** multiple trips for up to two years or until passport expiry, whichever comes first; up to 90 days per visit.
