The live form does not publish readable regexes, character limits or validation messages. This guide follows the public PIBA and government instructions instead.

## 1. Run the eligibility check

ETA-IL is only for citizens of visa-exempt countries. If PIBA says the passport needs a B/2 visa, leave the ETA application and use the visa route. If you already hold a valid Israeli visa or fall under another listed exemption, you may not need ETA-IL either.

## 2. Use the travel passport

Use the passport carried to Israel. The government entry page says it must remain valid for three months beyond arrival for an ETA traveller. A renewed passport requires a new ETA-IL.

## 3. Enter an email you control

The portal sends the receipt, payment receipt and decision by email. The approval message shows the ETA-IL number, validity and conditions. Keep it available during entry and the stay.

## 4. Choose the real visit purpose

ETA-IL covers short tourism or business visits of up to 90 days. Employment, study and other long stays require a visa. Do not select tourism to bypass a long-stay route.

## 5. Pay only ILS 25 on the government portal

The official fee is ILS 25 and non-refundable. Official notices say there is no express service. Confirm the host is `israel-entry.piba.gov.il` and save the payment receipt.

## 6. Review before submission

PIBA provides a final review step. Correct mistakes there. A submitted form cannot be edited; a mistake requires a new form and payment.

Many decisions are immediate, but allow up to 72 hours. If the result asks for more information, follow that email. If refused, use the help-center route instead of repeatedly filing new applications.
