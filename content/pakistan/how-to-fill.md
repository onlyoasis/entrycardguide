The portal may block automated sessions. Official notices and the mobile guide identify the application route, passport information, photographs and documents, but do not expose public text-field regexes, character limits or stable validation errors. This guide invents none and mounts no local validator.

## Section 1: Route and eligibility

**1. Visa route.** Check the passport before opening an application. Covered GCC citizens need no form. DGI&P currently places citizens of 120 countries on the VPA route.

**2. Application category.** Choose **Visa Prior to Arrival** and **Tourism** only when eligible. Do not confuse it with a regular tourist visa in the same portal.

## Section 2: Passport and documents

**3. Passport name.** Enter the full identity from the passport used for travel.

**4. Passport details.** Copy the number and document dates from that same passport.

**5. Nationality.** Choose passport nationality, not residence or departure country.

**6. Passport image.** Upload the passport document requested on the current screen. No unverified public pixel or file-size number is given here.

**7. Live photograph.** Complete the live capture step described in the official mobile guide and follow the current on-screen instructions.

## Section 3: Submit and receive

**8. Email and review.** Use a reachable account email. Review the route, category, passport and uploads before submission.

## After approval

Download the Visa Grant Notice. Keep it offline and read its validity, entries and permitted stay. A dashboard status alone is not the issued travel document.
