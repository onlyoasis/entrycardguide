Pakistan has three different tourist routes. Citizens of six GCC countries covered by the exemption need no ETA or visa. DGI&P currently says citizens of 120 countries can use the free **Visa Prior to Arrival** route. Other travellers must follow the applicable regular visa process.

{{< official-link site="pakistan.eligibility" >}}

The VPA and regular visa products share the Pakistan Online Visa System. Choose the product only after checking passport eligibility.

## Start here

### 1. Check exemption and VPA eligibility
Do not submit a paid regular application when the passport qualifies for visa-free entry or the free VPA route.

## Fast facts

- **Official portal:** `visa.nadra.gov.pk`.
- **Policy agency:** Directorate General of Immigration & Passports, Ministry of Interior.
- **VPA fee:** free for eligible applicants.
- **Timing:** obtain the Visa Grant Notice before travel; no universal processing time is claimed here.
- **Output:** Visa Grant Notice; read the issued document for entries and permitted stay.
- **Regular visa:** separate route with reciprocal fees.
