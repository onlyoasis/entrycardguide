Do not begin with the eVisa application. Morocco's official checker can return four different routes: no visa, a consular visa, AEVM or eVisa.

## Fast facts

- **Entry checker and eVisa portal:** `acces-maroc.ma`.
- **How to verify it:** follow the link from `diplomatie.ma`.
- **Agency:** Morocco Ministry of Foreign Affairs, African Cooperation and Moroccan Expatriates.
- **Standard:** MAD 770; published deadline 72 Moroccan working hours.
- **Express:** MAD 1,100; published deadline 24 Moroccan working hours.
- **Visa:** single entry, valid up to 180 days from issue, stay up to 30 days.
- **Passport:** ordinary passports only.
