## You may not need any visa

{{< official-link site="morocco.embassy_eligibility" >}}

The Moroccan embassy tells travellers to verify visa-exemption eligibility before applying. Use the live Accès Maroc checker with the passport nationality, residence, document type, trip purpose and supporting status.

If the result says no visa is required, stop. There is no eVisa form and no eVisa fee for that trip.

The same checker may instead direct the traveller to a consular visa or AEVM. Do not force those routes into the eVisa form.

## Verify the non-government-suffix portal

{{< official-link site="morocco.mfa_launch" >}}

`acces-maroc.ma` does not end in a government suffix. The safe verification path is to open the Ministry of Foreign Affairs page above and follow its Accès Maroc link. The Ministry says the platform was developed by engineers and technicians of the Department.

## Who can use eVisa

{{< official-link site="morocco.terms" >}}

The current briefing note defines three routes:

- **Category A:** listed nationalities using an ordinary passport valid at least 90 days from application.
- **Category B:** visa-required foreign citizens living in a listed country, with an ordinary passport valid at least 90 days and a qualifying residence card or permit valid at least 180 days.
- **Category C:** visa-required foreign citizens holding a qualifying multiple-entry non-electronic visa valid at least 90 days, plus an ordinary passport valid at least 90 days.

Use the live checker because the country lists can change.

## Price, processing, validity and stay

{{< official-link site="morocco.guide" >}}

- **Standard:** MAD 770; published deadline 72 working hours in Morocco.
- **Express:** MAD 1,100; published deadline 24 working hours in Morocco.
- **Entry:** single entry.
- **Validity:** up to 180 days from issue.
- **Stay:** no more than 30 days.
- **Purpose:** Tourism or Business.

Incomplete or false applications are outside the published deadline. A validated and paid request cannot be cancelled or refunded.

## Documents

The official note requires a recent 35 x 45 mm colour photo and clear colour copies of the ordinary passport identity and validity pages. Category B and C travellers add the exact residence permit or supporting visa used for eligibility. Attachments must be JPG, JPEG or PDF and no larger than 1 MB.

At the border, carry the eVisa, passport and the supporting status document used in the application.
