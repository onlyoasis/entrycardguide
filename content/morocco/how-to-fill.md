The public checker and official PDFs expose the route questions, required documents and upload limits. The authenticated text inputs do not publish regexes, character limits or official error strings. This page therefore mounts no local validator.

## 1. Run the eligibility checker

Enter the nationality, usual residence, document type, event answer, trip purpose and other valid-visa status. Follow the result exactly: no visa, consular visa, AEVM or eVisa.

## 2. Confirm the eVisa category

{{< official-link site="morocco.terms" >}}

Category A is nationality-based. Category B depends on residence and a qualifying permit valid at least 180 days. Category C depends on a qualifying multiple-entry non-electronic visa valid at least 90 days. All eVisa routes use an ordinary passport.

## 3. Verify email and open the secure link

Enter and confirm an email you control. The portal sends the secure application link there and uses the same address for later tracking.

## 4. Copy document data

Transcribe surname, given name, date of birth, nationality, document number and validity from the passport and any residence card or supporting visa used for eligibility.

## 5. Prepare the photo and passport copy

Use a good-quality 35 x 45 mm colour photo taken within the last 6 months. Attach clear colour copies of the ordinary passport pages showing identity and validity.

## 6. Add category evidence

Category B adds both sides of the qualifying residence card or permit. A receipt for an application, renewal or extension is not accepted. Category C adds the qualifying visa. Business applicants add evidence of the business purpose.

## 7. Keep attachments within the published limit

Scan attachments as JPG, JPEG or PDF, no larger than 1 MB. Review every field and file before validation because a validated, paid request cannot be cancelled or refunded.
