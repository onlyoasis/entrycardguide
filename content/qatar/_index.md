Qatar's current visitor system is passport-first. Qatar Tourism says citizens of more than 102 countries can use free entry. GCC citizens do not need a visa.

{{< official-link site="qatar.visa_guide" >}}

Travellers who need an entry visa can apply through Hayya. The Government Communications Office confirms that `hayya.qa` is the post-World Cup hub for tourist and event visas even though the domain is not a `.gov.qa` address.

## Fast facts

- **Free entry:** more than 102 passport countries, subject to the live route conditions.
- **GCC citizens:** no visa required.
- **Official e-Visa portal:** `hayya.qa`.
- **Government cross-check:** the State of Qatar's Government Communications Office links to Hayya.
- **Main tourist product:** Hayya Tourist Visa A1.
- **Other public categories:** A2, A3, A4 and F1 for their named status groups.
- **Tourist visa fee:** QAR 100.
- **Passport:** at least 3 months of validity from arrival or entry.
