The public checker exposes the route questions, and Hayya publishes its category descriptions. The account application does not expose later validation attributes or error text without a real application, so this guide does not invent them.

## 1. Enter passport nationality

Use the country that issued the passport. Both the Qatar Tourism checker and Hayya journey begin with nationality.

## 2. Separate GCC citizen from GCC resident

GCC citizens need no visa. A non-GCC citizen with current residence in Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Saudi Arabia or the UAE may instead qualify for A2.

## 3. Read the visa-free result

If the live result says Enter visa-free, read its passport, hotel and trip conditions. A qualifying traveller has no required paid Hayya tourist visa.

## 4. Answer residence and visa-status questions

The checker asks about GCC residence and residence in, or a valid visa for, specified countries. Use current status; an expired permit or visa is not the same answer.

## 5. Use the offered Hayya category

{{< official-link site="qatar.hayya" >}}

A1, A2, A3, A4 and F1 serve different groups. Do not choose A2 only because it sounds faster, or A1 when the live result says free entry.

## 6. Check passport validity

The Qatar Tourism checker requires at least 3 months of passport validity from arrival or entry.

## 7. Prepare the correct accommodation evidence

The A1 prompt requires a hotel reservation during the application. Some arrival routes require a Discover Qatar booking. Follow the instruction for the exact result.

## 8. Pay only after the route is clear

Qatar Tourism lists QAR 100 for the Hayya tourist visa and says A1 payment follows approval. Confirm the category and current amount before paying.
