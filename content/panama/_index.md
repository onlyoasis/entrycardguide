Panama's Digital Sworn Traveler Declaration is a customs declaration for goods and values when entering or leaving the country. The official portal supports Spanish and English. The declaration is free and may also be completed manually.

One declaration can cover a family group. Online filers use a photograph in place of a handwritten signature and must keep the QR response code for customs.

## Who has to file it

- **Must file:** every traveller entering or leaving Panama through any authorised route makes a traveller declaration.
- **Families:** a family group makes a single declaration. That is grouped filing, not an exemption.
- **By nationality:** there is no US or tourist-specific version. Every traveller uses the same free declaration, whatever the passport.

## Fast facts

- **Official portal:** `declaraciondeviajero.ana.gob.pa`.
- **Agency:** Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas.
- **Languages:** Spanish and English.
- **Cost:** free.
- **When:** digital filing and presentation follow the official 72-hour window.
- **Result:** QR response code shown to customs.
