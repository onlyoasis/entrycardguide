Ghana's Port Health Department runs the free Health Declaration at `ehds.ghs.gov.gh`. The portal says it is required for all arriving passengers. File it within 48 hours before you depart, and show the QR code to the Port Health officer when you land.

## Who has to file it

- **Must file:** "all arriving passengers", in the portal's words. The form makes no nationality exception.
- **Children:** "A parent or guardian completes it for a child under 12."
- **Since when:** the Ghana Health Service made it mandatory from 12 November 2026, as reported by the Daily Graphic. We did not find the release itself online.

Sources: {{< source-link site="ghana.ehds" text="Ghana Health Declaration portal" >}}, {{< source-link site="ghana.graphic_release" text="Daily Graphic, 7 Nov 2026" >}}.

## The fast facts

- **Official address:** `ehds.ghs.gov.gh`. Read the ending: **`.gov.gh`**.
- **Not official:** `ghs-hdf.org`. Facebook posts still give this address. On 18 November 2026 it opened as a site titled "Ghs Hdf - Wellness Wisdom Within", not a declaration form.
- **Cost:** none. The form's six steps include no payment step.
- **When:** within 48 hours before you depart.
- **What you get:** a QR code as soon as you submit. The portal can show your receipt again.
- **On arrival:** Port Health checks every passenger's temperature and reviews the declaration.
