## Quick answer

The Ghana Health Declaration is **free** and filed at **ehds.ghs.gov.gh**. The portal says it is required for **all arriving passengers**. Complete it **within 48 hours before you depart**. You get a QR code when you submit; show it to Port Health on arrival.

## ghs-hdf.org is not the form

Facebook posts about the new rule, some from November 2026, still point to `ghs-hdf.org`. On 18 November 2026 that address opened as a site titled "Ghs Hdf - Wellness Wisdom Within". It is not a declaration form, and it is not on a `.gov.gh` address.

The official form is at `ehds.ghs.gov.gh/declaration`. Read the address from the right: it must end in **`.gov.gh`**.

## Who has to file it

- **Every arriving passenger.** The portal's home page says "Required for all arriving passengers", and the form opens with "MANDATORY FOR ALL ARRIVING PASSENGERS". It makes no exception by nationality.
- **Children under 12:** "A parent or guardian completes it for a child under 12."
- **By air, land or sea:** the form has transport fields for each. The Ministry of Health statement reported by the Ghana News Agency named arrivals through Accra, Prempeh and Yakubu international airports.

## The 48-hour window

The form starts by asking for your departure date and says: "Complete this form within 48 hours before you depart." If you pick a date further away, it tells you which day to come back.

Example: flying out on Friday at 22:00, the form is open from Wednesday at 22:00.

## What the six steps ask

1. **Before you begin:** departure date and the rules above.
2. **Passenger information:** name, sex, date of birth, nationality, profession, ID type (passport, national ID card or other) and number, phone, how Port Health can reach you, email (optional).
3. **Travel information:** arrival date, country of departure, countries visited and countries transited in the last 21 days (or "Not Applicable"), point of entry, and flight, vessel or vehicle number.
4. **Health screening:** symptoms (fever, severe weakness, headache, vomiting or diarrhoea, unexplained bleeding) and possible exposure, such as attending a funeral or handling bushmeat in the last 21 days.
5. **Accommodation:** hotel or private residence, and the full address in Ghana.
6. **Declaration.**

None of the six steps asks for payment. False information or refusing Port Health instructions may lead to action under the Public Health Act 2012 (Act 851).

## On arrival

- Port Health checks every passenger's temperature and reviews the declaration.
- Travellers with symptoms, possible exposure or travel to an affected area may be taken aside for assessment.
- Port Health may contact you within 21 days of arrival.
- Lost the QR code? Use **Get my receipt again** on the portal.
- Unwell at the airport? Tell a Port Health officer. Ghana's emergency health line is 1800.
