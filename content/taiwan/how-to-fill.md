This guide follows the official English user manual. The live form publishes no regexes, character limits or validation messages, so none appear here. The one format the manual does state, DD/MM/YYYY for the departure date, is repeated below.

{{< official-link site="taiwan.user_manual" >}}

## Section 1: Account and document

**1. Email verification.** The flow opens with an email verification step and closes with a confirmation email or electronic document. Use an inbox you can open while travelling.

**2. Passport upload or manual entry.** The manual offers both. Either path must match the passport you actually carry, because inspection retrieves the record from a passport scan.

**3. Nationality.** Enter it as the travel document shows it. A transit point or country of residence is a different thing.

**4. Passport expiry.** Copy it from the data page. Issue date and expiry sit next to each other there, which is why they get swapped.

## Section 2: Applicant details

**5. Visa type and number.** Conditional. These appear only for travellers whose entry route requires them. If the form does not show the pair, nothing is missing.

**6. Occupation and job title.** Also conditional. When they appear, answer with the role you actually hold.

**7. Phone number.** Give a number reachable during the visit, not a line you switch off on landing.

## Section 3: Travel

**8. Arrival flight or vessel.** Use the operating service for the segment that lands in Taiwan, which is what appears on the manifest.

**9. Departure flight or vessel.** Asked in the same section, at submission time. Have the onward booking to hand before you start.

**10. Departure date.** The manual gives **DD/MM/YYYY**. This is the only date format the official document publishes, so it is the only one this guide states.

## Section 4: Stay

**11. Purpose of visit.** Choosing Other opens a reason field. Choosing to visit relatives opens a relative contact field. Pick the true purpose, then finish whatever it opens.

**12. Accommodation.** Give the address or property for the stay. A city name answers a narrower question than the one asked.

**13. Relative contact.** Appears only when the purpose is visiting relatives.

## Filing for a group

{{< official-link site="taiwan.deadline_notice" >}}

One person may file individually for up to 10 travellers, and the group-file import supports up to 16. Submit within 7 days before arrival.

## After submission

The system sends a confirmation email or electronic document. You do not need to show it at inspection, because scanning the passport retrieves the TWAC data. Carrying the same passport used in the submission is the whole requirement.
