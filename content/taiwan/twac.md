## The current portal

{{< official-link site="taiwan.twac" >}}

The arrival card is submitted at `twac.immigration.gov.tw`, on a government `.gov.tw` host. The older `niaspeedy.immigration.gov.tw` address is outdated; if a guide sends you there, check its date before trusting anything else on the page.

Paper cards ended on **1 October 2025**. Since then the arrival card is online only.

{{< official-link site="taiwan.launch_notice" >}}

## It is free

{{< official-link site="taiwan.free_notice" >}}

The agency has published a notice confirming the arrival card is submitted free of charge through the official channel. A page that quotes a price for this form is charging for something the agency does not.

## The window is 7 days

{{< official-link site="taiwan.deadline_notice" >}}

The current submission window is **within 7 days before arrival**, extended in July 2026. That is more room than travellers had before, and it is the reason a guide written last year may tell you to wait.

## Who submits it

The official homepage names three groups:

- foreign nationals **without** an Alien Resident Certificate, Resident Visa or Diplomatic ID;
- specified **Mainland, Hong Kong and Macao permit holders**;
- **nationals without household registration** who do not hold Taiwan-area residence certificates.

If you hold one of the residence documents named in the first group, the homepage is telling you the arrival card is not your step.

## Filing for other people

One person may file individually for up to **10 travellers**. The group-file import supports up to **16**. A family or a small tour does not need one account per person.

## What the form collects

{{< official-link site="taiwan.user_manual" >}}

The official English manual names the items: email verification, passport upload or manual entry, nationality, passport expiry, conditional visa type and number, conditional occupation and job title, email and phone, arrival and departure flight or vessel, departure date in **DD/MM/YYYY**, purpose, accommodation, a relative contact when the purpose is visiting relatives, and a reason when the purpose is Other.

→ [Walk through all 13 items](/taiwan/how-to-fill/)

## After you submit

The system sends a confirmation email or electronic document. You do not have to show it at inspection: scanning the passport retrieves the TWAC record. Carry the same passport you used in the submission, and that step takes care of itself.
