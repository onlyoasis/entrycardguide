The Taiwan Arrival Card replaced paper cards on **1 October 2025**. It is submitted online, and it is free.

{{< official-link site="taiwan.twac" >}}

The current submission window is **within 7 days before arrival**, extended in July 2026. Older guides describing a shorter window, or pointing at `niaspeedy.immigration.gov.tw`, are out of date.

## Who has to file it

- **Must file:** foreign visitors who do not hold a Taiwan residence certificate, diplomatic ID card or resident visa; Hong Kong and Macao residents entering on a multiple-entry permit; mainland Chinese residents entering on a multiple-entry tourist permit; ROC nationals without household registration who lack a residence certificate.
- **Does not file:** ROC nationals with household registration; foreigners holding one of the documents above; Hong Kong and Macao residents on a single-entry permit, online entry permit, landing visa or ROC residence certificate.
- **By nationality:** US, Australian and Canadian visitors follow the foreign-visitor rule. For Hong Kong and Macao residents, the permit type decides it.

Sources: {{< source-link site="taiwan.egov_filing_scope" text="Government portal notice" >}} and {{< source-link site="taiwan.launch_notice" text="NIA launch notice" >}}. Whether you also need an entry permit or visa is a separate question.

## Fast facts

- **Official portal:** `twac.immigration.gov.tw`, under the government `.gov.tw` domain.
- **Agency:** National Immigration Agency, Ministry of the Interior.
- **Cost:** free.
- **When:** within 7 days before arrival.
- **Since:** online only from 1 October 2025.
- **Groups:** one person may file individually for up to 10 travellers, and the group-file import supports up to 16.
- **At inspection:** no printout needed. Scanning the passport retrieves the TWAC record.
