In March 2026, Thailand's Royal Immigration Bureau stated that about [10% of foreign arrivals](/thailand/tdac/#the-problem) had paid an unofficial website to submit the TDAC — a form that is, and has always been, free.

That pattern repeats across countries with digital arrival cards, customs declarations, and e-visa flows. There is one official government URL. There are five to fifteen commercial sites buying ads against searches for that URL, charging USD 20 to 90 to fill an 8-minute form.

We list the real URL and explain every field, locally. We do not publish lists of third-party companies. The whole site is on GitHub, and every URL has a `last_verified` date.
