The live portal publishes field labels, date order and image constraints. It does not expose safe public regexes or character limits for passport names, numbers, addresses or contacts. This guide documents the executable image rules but mounts no incomplete local validator.

## Section 1: Route and eligibility

**1. Visa-free status.** Check the current MFA list. A confirmed visa-free traveller needs no eVisa form or payment.

**2. eVisa eligibility.** If a visa is required, confirm the passport in current Appendix 1. Other passports use a foreign-mission route.

**3. Travel document.** Select the citizenship and document type that will be shown at the border. The eVisa is tied to that passport.

**4. Entry type.** Choose single, double or multiple entry. The current fees are USD 20, USD 35 and USD 50.

**5. Purpose and dates.** Choose tourism and a stay of no more than 30 days. The portal redirects non-tourism invitations to the MFA visa route.

## Section 2: Personal and passport data

**6. Passport name.** Enter surname, given name and patronymic in English from the valid travel document.

**7. Birth details.** The live English label displays **Date of birth (DD.MM.YYYY)**. Use the official control and copy the passport place of birth.

**8. Passport details.** Copy the series and number, issue date and expiration date from the same document.

## Section 3: Residence and trip

**9. Residence and contact.** Enter the permanent address and a reachable mobile or land-line number.

**10. Work and stay.** Prepare the work or study and position details, plus the preliminary hotel or residence in Uzbekistan.

## Section 4: Images and activation

**11. Face photo.** Use a 35 × 45 mm image at 300 dpi. The current official error requires JPG or JPEG below 1024 KB, and the face must meet the portal's ICAO guidance.

**12. Passport copy.** Upload a readable data-page image no larger than the published 500 KB maximum.

**13. Email activation.** Enter a reachable email and submit the digital code sent there. The final decision and approved PDF use the same inbox.

## After approval

Carry the PDF in printed or electronic form with the application passport. Confirm the permitted entries, 30-day maximum stay and 90-day entry window on the issued document.
