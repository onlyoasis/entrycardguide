The official brochure identifies four sections but does not publish the live form's regexes, character limits or error messages. Follow the controls visible on the portal.

## Before starting

Open D'Viajeros during the seven days before travel. Have the passport, confirmed arrival itinerary, accommodation and accessible email ready.

If a tourist visa is required, obtain the eVisa code first. D'Viajeros is free; the eVisa is a separate application and fee.

## Traveller and immigration information

Use the passport carried on the trip. The official tourism page says it must remain valid for six months from entry. The eVisa, when required, must be linked to the same passport.

Copy the current arrival and accommodation details. Enter the emailed eVisa code only in the visa field shown by the live portal.

## Customs and health information

Answer for the current trip and the baggage actually carried. Do not copy answers from an earlier visit. The public brochure confirms these two sections but does not publish their live wording or validation rules.

## Save the result

After completion, D'Viajeros emails a PDF containing the QR code. Save it offline and, if practical, print it. Show it with the separate entry documents when asked on arrival.

If the email does not arrive, first check spam and confirm the address entered. Do not pay a site to “unlock” a free D'Viajeros QR.
