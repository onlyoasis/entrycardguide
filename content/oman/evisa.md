## You may not need an eVisa

Start with the Foreign Ministry's current page, updated in January 2026.

{{< official-link site="oman.visa_exemption" >}}

It lists more than 100 countries and regions in two groups for stays up to **14 days**:

- first-group passports can use the exemption when the published trip conditions are met;
- second-group passports also need a qualifying visa or residence status described on the page;
- citizens of Bahrain, Kuwait, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and the UAE do not need a visa.

The 14-day exemption cannot be extended or converted into a residence visa. It also requires a passport valid for at least 6 months, a return ticket, confirmed hotel booking, health insurance and enough money for the stay.

If this route covers the trip, do not submit or pay for an eVisa.

## Use the eligibility wizard next

{{< official-link site="oman.eligibility" >}}

The Royal Oman Police asks for passport nationality and whether the applicant resides in a GCC country. Its result supplies:

- eligible visa subtypes;
- fee details;
- permitted stay;
- conditions for the selected route.

Do not copy a price or duration from another passport. If the wizard does not offer an unsponsored visa, the answer is not to choose a different nationality.

## Official application

{{< official-link site="oman.evisa" >}}

Tourist applicants register or log in before starting. The portal is run by the Royal Oman Police. The FAQ lists unsponsored tourist, sponsored tourist, GCC-resident tourist and express visas, so the product and charge depend on the traveller's result.

## Timing and validity

{{< official-link site="oman.faq" >}}

The ROP recommends lodging at least **4 days before arrival** and says processing times vary. It does not promise approval within 4 days.

For an unsponsored visa, the FAQ says it is valid for use for **one month after issue**. That is the window to enter, not a universal stay length. Read the stay duration shown for the exact subtype.

## Files and delivery

The normal passport should be valid for at least **6 months** before entry. The FAQ caps both passport and picture files at **512 KB**.

After successful payment and submission, an approved electronic visa is sent to the registered email. Payment does not guarantee approval.
