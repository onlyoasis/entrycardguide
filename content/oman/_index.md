Oman's current entry page places many short tourist trips outside the paid eVisa route. More than 100 countries and regions appear in two exemption groups for stays up to 14 days. GCC citizens do not need a visa.

{{< official-link site="oman.visa_exemption" >}}

The second group has extra visa or residence conditions, and the 14-day stay cannot be extended or converted. Travellers outside an exemption should use the Royal Oman Police eligibility wizard.

## Fast facts

- **14-day exemption:** more than 100 countries and regions, split into two groups.
- **GCC citizens:** no visa required.
- **Official application:** `evisa.rop.gov.om`.
- **Operator:** Royal Oman Police.
- **Fee and permitted stay:** shown for the exact eligible visa subtype.
- **Recommended timing:** apply at least 4 days before arrival when a visa is needed.
- **Passport:** at least 6 months of validity before entry.
- **Uploads:** passport and picture files no larger than 512 KB each.
