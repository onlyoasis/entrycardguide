The public first screen exposes citizenship, travel-document type, purpose, insurance and its document checklist. Later personal-information validation attributes and error text were not readable, so this guide does not invent them.

## 1. Check the visa-free ordinance

{{< official-link site="georgia.visa_free" >}}

Find the passport country in the current annex. If it is listed and the trip fits the rule, you may enter and stay without a visa for one full year. Stop there; no e-Visa form is needed.

## 2. Select citizenship from the travel passport

Choose the country shown on the passport you will present at the border, not your residence. Dual citizens should use the same document for the application and the trip.

## 3. Select the actual document type

The portal shows eligibility after the citizenship and travel-document choices. A person who needs a visa does not automatically qualify for this online route.

## 4. Choose the real short-stay purpose

The portal limits e-Visas to visits of less than 90 days. Select the purpose that matches the supporting document you can show at the border.

## 5. Check the passport conditions

The FAQ requires at least 3 months of validity after the requested visa expires, at least 2 free pages and an issue date within the previous 10 years.

## 6. Use a compliant recent photo

Use a front-facing photo taken within 6 months. The FAQ rejects mobile-device photos, snapshots, full-length photos and images copied or scanned from another official document.

## 7. Prepare every upload

The live checklist asks for the passport scanned into one PDF, accommodation, a round ticket, travel insurance, financial means and travel-purpose evidence. Add the supporting-document copy when it applies.

## 8. Watch the email clock

Verify the first email within 1 hour. After the payment notice, pay within 5 hours or the application is cancelled.

## 9. Complete mandatory DuVerify

After the first application and consular payment, open the DuVerify email and finish that step within 24 hours. The live page lists an additional USD 15 fee per applicant.
