Georgia is visa-free for citizens of many countries. The current government ordinance says citizens of countries in its annex may enter and stay without a visa for one full year.

{{< official-link site="georgia.visa_free" >}}

If your passport country is listed and your trip fits the rule, there is no e-Visa form to file. Travellers who still need a short-stay visa should use only the Ministry of Foreign Affairs portal.

## Fast facts

- **Visa-free route:** citizens of countries in the current annex may stay without a visa for one full year.
- **Official application:** `evisa.gov.ge`.
- **Operator:** Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Georgia.
- **Published charges:** USD 20 plus a 2% service fee, then USD 15 for mandatory DuVerify.
- **Decision time:** not less than 5 working days.
- **Visa validity and permitted stay:** depend on the passport selected in the official application.
- **Output:** an e-Visa PDF sent by email; print it and carry the supporting border documents.
