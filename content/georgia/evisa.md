## You may not need this visa

Do not start at the payment screen. Start with the current government ordinance.

{{< official-link site="georgia.visa_free" >}}

Citizens of countries in its annex may enter and stay in Georgia without a visa for one full year. Check the exact passport country in the current text. If it is covered and your trip fits the rule, there is no e-Visa application to submit and no visa fee to pay.

## Who should use the e-Visa portal

The Ministry describes e-Visa as a short-stay route. The live application says it is only for visits of less than 90 days and makes eligibility depend on citizenship and travel-document type.

{{< official-link site="georgia.evisa" >}}

Select the passport you will actually use. Visa validity and permitted stay vary by passport, so rely on the result shown by the application rather than a duration copied from another traveller.

## The current fee has two parts

The official FAQ lists:

- **USD 20 visa fee**;
- **2% service fee**.

{{< official-link site="georgia.faq" >}}

The live application adds a second mandatory step after the first application and consular payment:

- complete **DuVerify within 24 hours** using the emailed link;
- pay an additional **USD 15 per applicant**.

Stopping after the USD 20 payment does not complete the current process.

## Processing and email deadlines

The FAQ says a decision takes **not less than 5 working days**. It also gives two earlier deadlines:

- verify the first application email within **1 hour**;
- pay within **5 hours** after the payment notice.

Allow more than the stated minimum. The site does not promise a maximum decision time.

## Documents to prepare

The live application lists a photo, the passport scanned into one PDF, accommodation, a round ticket, travel insurance, proof of financial means and proof of travel purpose. A supporting-document copy is added when applicable.

At the border, be ready to present the passport and the same trip evidence. An e-Visa does not replace those checks.

→ [Prepare every application item](/georgia/how-to-fill/)

## What you receive

The Ministry emails the e-Visa as a PDF. Print it and bring it with the same passport used for the application. If any e-Visa detail does not match the passport, the FAQ says the document is invalid and the fee is not refunded.
