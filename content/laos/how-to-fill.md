The live application shows required labels, DD-MM-YYYY prompts and 2 MB upload limits. It does not publish text-field regexes, character limits or official error messages, so this guide invents none.

## Section 1: Route and documents

**1. Eligibility.** Check the official passport list before paying. Visa exemption, eVisa and visa on arrival are different routes.

**2. Face photo.** Use a 4 x 6 cm colour image taken within 6 months. Plain white background, no shadows, neutral expression and both eyes open. Upload JPG, JPEG or PNG no larger than 2 MB.

**3. Passport image.** Upload the whole bio page, including the two MRZ lines at the bottom. Keep every edge visible. The same 2 MB JPG/JPEG/PNG limit applies.

## Section 2: Passport data

**4. Surname.** Copy it from the passport used for this application.

**5. Given names.** Include every given name shown there, including a middle name.

**6. Date of birth.** The live form displays **DD-MM-YYYY**. Use its date control.

**7. Passport number.** Use the document you will carry. The Approval Letter is valid only with that passport.

**8. Passport validity.** Only ordinary passports qualify. The terms require more than 6 months of validity from arrival and at least one blank page.

## Section 3: Trip

**9. Port of entry.** Choose one of the 9 designated eVisa ports. Arriving elsewhere means the eVisa cannot be used and the fee is not refunded.

**10. Intended entry date.** Use DD-MM-YYYY. The FAQ says the date may be no more than 60 days from application and the system requires at least 5 days before arrival.

**11. Address during the visit.** Enter the hotel or local address in Laos. Keep the booking available for a spelling check.

## After submission

Normal processing is 3 working days after a complete application and payment. Check status with the application ID and passport number. When approved, print the Approval Letter and carry it with the same passport to a designated port.
