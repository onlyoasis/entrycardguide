The Lao eVisa is a **single-entry Tourist Visa**. It is not the same thing as a visa on arrival, and it only works at the 9 ports named by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

{{< official-link site="laos.evisa" >}}

The government portal calculates the fee after you select the passport. It does not publish one amount that is safe to quote for every traveller, and the fee is non-refundable.

## Fast facts

- **Official application:** `laoevisa.gov.la/application`, under the government `.gov.la` domain.
- **Agency:** Consular Department, Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Lao PDR.
- **Visa type:** Tourist Visa (T-B3), single entry only.
- **Fee:** calculated by passport on the official portal; non-refundable.
- **Processing:** normally 3 working days after a complete application and payment.
- **Stay:** 30 days; the Approval Letter is valid for 60 days after issue.
- **Entry:** one of 9 designated ports, with a printed Approval Letter and the same passport used in the application.
