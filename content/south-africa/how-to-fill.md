The cards above group 12 preparation items into four guide sections. They do not count all official questions or steps. Examples show what to check against your documents and circumstances, not an official acceptance or rejection test. No personal details were submitted for this guide.

## 1. Declaration and transport

Confirm whether you are entering or leaving South Africa and select your transport and port. Read the **Full** and **Nil** descriptions. No declarable goods means Nil when its conditions apply; it does not remove the filing requirement.

Prepare the flight, train, vessel or vehicle identifiers for your mode. Do not invent a flight number for a land journey.

## 2. Identity

Have your travel document ready: type, number, issuing country, nationality, name and birth date. Compare the details with the document you will present. Do not assume a South African ID number applies to every foreign visitor.

The current first screen shows a birth-date hint of **CCYY/MM/DD**. Use its date control and your real birth date. This hint is not proof of every accepted input format. Single names and special-character branches have not been verified for this guide; seek SARS guidance if needed.

## 3. Contact and accommodation

Prepare an accessible email. Provide your mobile details if you have a mobile phone. Where applicable, the rules permit a port email arranged by Customs; ask for assistance rather than making one up. Occupation is conditional on applicability.

For **entry**, use the address where you will stay in South Africa. For **departure**, rule 15.04(a)(iv) asks for your accommodation in the destination country. Check street, city and postal details against your booking; a fixed South African-address heading in a screen or guide does not override the direction-specific rule.

## 4. Journey, companions and goods

State your actual purpose and whether you travel personally or for an entity. Prepare entity details when that applies. Use current travel dates and include transit points only where relevant.

If you have companions, prepare their document details and make sure each traveller has declaration coverage. Children and infants also need coverage; a parent or legal guardian can submit for them. Do not assume the companion list alone completes every person's declaration.

Answer the goods and currency questions truthfully. Supply item descriptions, amounts, values, sources or serial numbers when prompted. Check current official currency rules; this page does not present a single threshold covering every situation.

## Submit within the correct window

Submit within **24 hours before departure**. For an inbound connection, count from the last leg directly to South Africa. For departure, count from leaving South Africa. This is not an arrival-time window. Railway and non-designated-place procedures are addressed in the official FAQ.

Update changed details before proceeding through Customs. Electronic filing failures can receive assistance where available; paper forms are limited to the official exceptions, not an unrestricted alternative.

## Save the confirmation for Customs

SARS says it emails confirmation and port instructions. Save the confirmation on your phone or print it. This guide has not tested a completed submission or email delivery.

On arrival, complete immigration first, then follow the confirmation to Customs. On departure, complete the instructed Customs matters before immigration. **The SARS confirmation is for Customs and does not replace immigration permission.**
