Jordan has two separate things people confuse. The **visa** is issued by the Interior Ministry, and the single-entry tourist visa costs **JD 40 for one month** on arrival at the airport. The **Jordan Pass** is a Ministry of Tourism package that includes over 40 sites and removes that JD 40 fee under conditions.

{{< official-link site="jordan.jordan_pass" >}}

The pass is not a visa. Its own FAQ says so: it only waives the visa fee. You still get the visa at the border, and officers check the pass against your passport.

## Fast facts

- **Official purchase site:** `jordanpass.jo`. The domain is not a government suffix; the operator is the Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities, and `mota.gov.jo/En/Pages/General_Information` links to it.
- **Prices:** JD 70 (Jordan Wanderer, 1 Petra day), JD 75 (Jordan Explorer, 2 consecutive days), JD 80 (Jordan Expert, 3 consecutive days).
- **Visa fee it waives:** the JD 40 single-entry visa for one month on arrival, if bought before arrival and the stay is 2 whole nights (3 days).
- **Visa on arrival:** Queen Alia Airport and Sheikh Hussein Bridge. Wadi Araba grants it to pass holders. Aqaba Airport needs no visa for non-restricted nationalities.
- **Restricted nationalities:** 53 listed by the Interior Ministry as needing prior approval.
- **Interior Ministry e-visa:** an account login at `eservices.moi.gov.jo/MOI_EVISA`, for applications filed from outside the Kingdom, with a JD 2 service allowance and JD 1000 guarantees per invited person.
