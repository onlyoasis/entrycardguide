The public screen and official guidance show the field labels and preparation items. They do not expose readable text-field regexes, character limits or official validation errors. This guide invents none and mounts no local validator.

## Section 1: Route and eligibility

**1. Visa route.** Confirm visa-on-arrival eligibility first. Indian citizens need no visa; the current restricted list must use an advance route.

**2. Entry point.** Choose the airport or land office where you will actually enter and receive the visa.

**3. Passport country.** Select the country that issued the travel passport, not the country of residence. Gratis treatment follows the eligible category.

## Section 2: Passport and identity

**4. Passport number.** Use the passport that will be presented at the immigration desk.

**5. Date of birth.** The live first screen says **Date of Birth (AD)** and uses day, month and year choices. Enter the Gregorian date from the passport.

**6. Full name.** Copy the complete passport name. Do not shorten it to a booking nickname.

**7. Passport validity.** The Nepal Embassy in Beijing says more than 6 months is required for processing.

**8. Digital photo.** Prepare a recent passport-size digital photograph. No verified public pixel or file-size limit is claimed here.

## Section 3: Stay and contact

**9. Addresses.** Enter the detailed permanent address and the full hotel or local address in Nepal.

**10. Contact and duration.** Give a working phone number and email. Choose 15, 30 or 90 days before payment.

## After submission

Save or print the barcode receipt before closing the page. It remains valid for 15 days. Present the form, payment receipt and passport at the immigration desk; the officer issues the visa there.
