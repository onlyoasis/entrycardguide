Use the official `.go.kr` portal. Do not paste e-Arrival Card data into K-ETA, Q-Code, or a visa-service page by mistake.

## Do you need to file?

Most foreign visitors do. The portal's {{< source-link site="korea.subject_guide" text="declaration guide" >}} lists both sides:

- **File it:** Korean visa holders; K-ETA-exempt travellers (citizens of countries temporarily exempt from K-ETA, under-17s and over-65s from K-ETA countries, diplomatic and service passports); US forces under SOFA; UN Laissez-Passer and APEC Business Travel Card holders.
- **Skip it:** Korean citizens (dual nationals enter on the Korean passport); holders of a valid Korean residence card, permanent resident card or Overseas Korean resident card; holders of a valid K-ETA; group electronic visa holders; foreign flight crew.
- **K-ETA plus a visa:** a K-ETA holder who enters on a visa still files the e-Arrival Card.
- **Transit:** a passenger who transfers within 24 hours without entering Korea does not file.

Not sure? The portal's Navigator asks which of visa, K-ETA and residence card you hold.

## When to file

From 3 days before arrival, counted in Korea Standard Time. If you land on 31 January, you can file on 29, 30 or 31 January. The portal's home page shows the three open dates.

You can also file after landing, any time before immigration. If you have not filed, you can still fill in an e-Arrival Card or a paper card before the immigration counter.

A card expires 72 hours after submission, and it is used up once you pass immigration. Each entry needs a new card.

## The five steps

1. **Terms and email.** Agree to the terms and type the representative's email twice. Someone aged 14 or younger cannot be the representative. Since 6 May 2026 the portal also asks for a verification code when you submit.
2. **Passport.** Photograph or upload the passport data page and the portal reads it. The image must include the two machine-readable lines at the bottom. Check every value it fills in: surname, given name, date of birth, nationality, passport number, sex and expiry date. If the scan fails, type the details yourself.
3. **Travel details.** Choose AIR or SEA, enter the arrival date and flight number (for example KE000), and press the flight lookup to fill in where you are flying from. Do the same for departure; the departure flight number is optional. Then pick the purpose of entry, enter your place of stay and contact number, and pick your occupation. A valid Korean visa number can pull in the stay address and contact from your visa application.
4. **Check.** Correct anything that does not match your passport.
5. **Submit.** Save or print the PDF with your issue number. The same details go to the email address you entered.

If the flight lookup finds nothing, type the country and city yourself. **Temporary save** keeps a draft for 72 hours; reopen it from the home page.

The steps above follow the portal's {{< source-link site="korea.procedure" text="declaration procedure" >}} and its {{< source-link site="korea.user_guide" text="PDF user guide" >}}.

## Address in Korea

Enter the main place you will stay. With no main place, enter where you stay first after arriving.

A same-day transit passenger who passes immigration, for example to collect bags, enters the airport's address:

| Incheon Terminal 1 | 272 Gonghang-ro, Jung-gu, Incheon (22382) |

To enter the address, use the address lookup: search by road-name address or the 5-digit postcode, in Korean or English, then add the room or detail line. The user guide's example is 151 Mokdongdong-ro, Yangcheon-gu, Seoul (postcode 08013).

## Contact number and occupation

- **Contact number:** it sits with your place of stay, and the box asks for a contact number in Korea. A valid Korean visa can fill it in for you.
- **Occupation:** a required drop-down list. If nothing fits, choose "Other" and type it in; the user guide's examples are athlete and religious worker. The guide does not publish the full list.
- **Purpose of entry:** also a required list. "Other" opens a box for details, such as academic research or a concert.

## Departure date and flight

No onward booking yet? Enter an approximate departure date. The departure flight number is optional. The officer may still ask about your departure plans.

## Date of birth without a day or month

Some passports print only the year, such as XX/XX/1999. Scan the passport so the portal reads it. If you must type it, the official FAQ says to enter `1999.00.00`. The checker on this page only accepts full YYYY-MM-DD dates.

## Families and groups

- **Individual form:** 1 to 9 people, on a computer or a phone browser. There is no app.
- **Group form:** 2 to 1,000 people, computer only. Everyone in it must share the arrival date and flight number (or ship name).
- **Children:** the law expects each traveller to fill in their own card and lets someone else do it when they cannot. A parent can file for a minor child; check every detail.
- **Same details as the representative:** when you add a traveller, "Same as representative" copies the representative's entry, exit, purpose and stay details. After that, change those details on the representative only; each added traveller still picks their own occupation.
- **Issue numbers:** all of them go to the representative's email. If you entered each traveller's email, each person also gets their own.

A travel agency can file for a group tourist who cannot do it themselves. The answers still count as the traveller's own, and wrong details can delay entry or lead to refusal.

## After you submit

- You do not need to show the PDF or the email at immigration. If your details were correct, the officer can see your card.
- If a detail was wrong, the officer may ask for documents with the issue number.
- Lost the issue number? You do not need to file again. Use email verification on Check/Edit.
- Filing does not enrol you in the automated immigration gates, and it does not replace customs or quarantine declarations.

## Changing a submitted card

Open Check/Edit with your issue number or email verification, before you reach immigration. You can change your place of stay, purpose of entry, occupation and departure date. The arrival date can only be changed within 3 days of the first submission.

## Language, email and phone problems

- **Languages:** Korean, English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Thai, Vietnamese and Russian.
- **Email delays:** the portal's {{< source-link site="korea.email_notice" text="notice" >}} names Gmail, Outlook.com, Hotmail.com and QQ.com. Use another address, or save the PDF as soon as you submit.
- **Phones in mainland China:** the portal says {{< source-link site="korea.china_access_notice" text="access from smartphones in China" >}} can be unstable and recommends a desktop or laptop.
- **Help:** earrivalcard@korea.kr (answers in Korean and English), or the Immigration Contact Center on 1345 (+82-2-1345 from abroad), weekdays 09:00 to 22:00, with Chinese available after 18:00.

## Practical notes

File with the passport and flight you will actually use. If a site asks for a K-ETA or visa fee, you are not just filing the free e-Arrival Card.
