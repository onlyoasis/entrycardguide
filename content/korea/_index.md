Korea e-Arrival Card is the official electronic arrival-card portal at `e-arrivalcard.go.kr`. It is separate from K-ETA, visas, and Q-Code health declarations.

## Who has to file it

- **Must file:** visitors entering on a Korean visa, and K-ETA-exempt visitors. A K-ETA holder who enters on a visa still files.
- **Does not file:** Korean citizens; holders of a valid Korean residence card, permanent resident card or Overseas Korean resident card; valid K-ETA holders; foreign flight attendants; the group-visa tourists named by the Korea Immigration Service.
- **By nationality:** there is no US, Canadian or Hong Kong version. Your document decides it: with a valid K-ETA you skip the card, without one you file.

## The fast facts

- **Official URL:** `www.e-arrivalcard.go.kr`. The portal says it is the only official site.
- **Cost:** free.
- **When:** from 3 days before arrival (Korea Standard Time), or after landing before immigration. A card expires 72 hours after submission.
- **Who skips it:** Korean citizens, Korean residence card holders, and holders of a valid K-ETA (unless entering on a visa), among others.
- **Languages:** Korean, English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Thai, Vietnamese, Russian.
- **At immigration:** you do not need to show the PDF or email.
- **What it is not:** K-ETA, visa approval, or Q-Code.
