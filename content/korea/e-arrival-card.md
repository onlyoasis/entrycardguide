## What the e-Arrival Card is

Korea e-Arrival Card is the online version of the paper arrival card, on the official `.go.kr` portal. It has the same effect as the paper card.

It is free.

It is not K-ETA. It is not a visa. It is not Q-Code. Those systems answer different questions.

{{< validator country="korea" >}}

## The only official site

The portal opens with its own warning: `www.e-arrivalcard.go.kr` is the only official e-Arrival Card website, and any site asking for payment information is not an official service. Its {{< source-link site="korea.fake_site_notice" text="notice on fake sites" >}} says look-alike sites use similar names and charge fees. Check for the `go.kr` domain and the government logo before you type anything.

## Who files it

Most foreign visitors: visa holders and K-ETA-exempt travellers, among others. Korean citizens, holders of a valid Korean residence card, holders of a valid K-ETA, group electronic visa holders and foreign flight crew do not. The full lists are in the [field guide](/korea/how-to-fill/#do-you-need-to-file).

## Timing, people and languages

- **When:** from 3 days before arrival (Korea Standard Time), or after landing before immigration.
- **Validity:** 72 hours after submission, for one entry.
- **How many people:** 1 to 9 on the individual form (computer or phone browser); 2 to 1,000 on the group form (computer only).
- **Languages:** Korean, English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Thai, Vietnamese and Russian.
- **Result:** a PDF with your issue number, also emailed. You do not need to show it at immigration.

## System mix-ups

Paid travel sites often blur Korea entry requirements into one product. Keep them separate:

- K-ETA or visa status decides whether you can travel. K-ETA is for nationals of visa-free countries and costs 10,000 won; the e-Arrival Card is free.
- e-Arrival Card is arrival-card data. A K-ETA that is still valid replaces it, unless you enter on a visa.
- Q-Code is health/quarantine data. The e-Arrival Card does not replace customs or quarantine declarations.

## Common questions

### What is the official Korea e-Arrival Card website?
www.e-arrivalcard.go.kr. The portal itself says it is the only official site, and that any site asking for payment information is not official.

### Is Korea e-Arrival Card free?
Yes. The official e-Arrival Card filing is free.

### When can I submit the Korea e-Arrival Card?
From 3 days before arrival, in Korea Standard Time, and any time after landing before immigration. A card expires 72 hours after submission.

### Does the e-Arrival Card website support Chinese?
Yes. The portal works in 7 languages: Korean, English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Thai, Vietnamese and Russian.

### Is e-Arrival Card the same as K-ETA or Q-Code?
No. K-ETA, visas, Q-Code, and e-Arrival Card are separate systems.

### Do K-ETA holders need an e-Arrival Card?
No, not with a K-ETA that is still valid. A K-ETA holder who enters on a visa still files the e-Arrival Card.

### Do I still need a paper arrival card?
No. The e-Arrival Card has the same effect as the paper card. If you did not file online, you can still fill in an e-Arrival Card or a paper card before immigration.
