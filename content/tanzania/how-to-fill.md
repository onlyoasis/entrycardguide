## 1. Check exemption and referral status

{{< official-link site="tanzania.guidelines" >}}

Use the passport you will present. A visa-exempt traveller should not open a paid application. A referral-case traveller must apply online, wait for approval and allow at least 2 months.

## 2. Choose the visa by purpose

Ordinary Visa is the normal tourism route except for United States passport holders. They use Multiple Visa. Transit Visa is only for onward transit and requires destination visa and/or onward-ticket evidence.

## 3. Save the start-screen recovery data

The first page asks for email, passport number, issuing country, a security question and its answer. Save the resulting application ID with the exact question and answer. The email and answer each allow no more than 100 characters.

## 4. Check the passport before paying

The passport needs at least 6 months of validity and one unused visa page. Use the same passport number and issuing country throughout the application and at travel.

## 5. Prepare the photo and travel proof

Use a colour full-face photo on a plain background. The official limit is 45 mm by 35 mm, 500 KB, JPG or JPEG. Ordinary applicants prepare the passport bio page and return ticket. Transit applicants prepare onward evidence.

## 6. Allow the stated processing time

The normal published period is within 10 days, excluding referral cases. Wait for the approval notification or download the grant notice through the official status page before travel.
