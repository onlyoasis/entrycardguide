## Quick answer

Vietnam's Pre-Arrival Information (PAI) form is **free** and is filed at **prearrival.immigration.gov.vn**. The portal accepts it when your expected arrival date is **today or within the next 2 days**. After you submit, you get a QR code to show at immigration.

It is not a visa. If your passport needs one, the [Vietnam e-visa](/vietnam/evisa/) is a separate application with a government fee of USD $25 or $50.

The notices disagree on wording. The April U.S. notice said SGN only. Vietnam's own embassies call it encouraged rather than required at the other four airports. The June U.S. notice says all airports. Plan as if it is required wherever you land by air: it is free and takes a few minutes. The June notice adds that the form is also available on arrival, by QR code or on paper at the airport.

## When to file: two days, not 72 hours

Two versions circulate:

- **"Not more than three days before arrival"** or "72 hours": the wording in the U.S. Embassy and Vietnamese embassy notices.
- **"Today or within the next 2 days"**: the portal's own error message for the arrival date, read on 18 November 2026. Another message in the same file allows "yesterday, today, or within the next two days".

The portal is what accepts or rejects your form. Example: landing on 20 November, you can file from 18 November. On 17 November the date is refused.

## Who files it

- **Must file:** all international passengers who do not hold a Vietnamese passport (U.S. Embassy, 17 June 2026).
- **SGN rule from April:** foreign passport holders, and overseas Vietnamese entering with a visa.
- **Transit:** passengers in transit are exempt, per the Singapore Consulate notice for SGN.
- **Vietnamese citizens:** the portal has a separate version for them. This guide does not cover it.

## What the form asks

Read from the portal's English labels on 18 November 2026. A CAPTCHA comes before the form; you can scan the passport photo page to fill some fields.

- **Passenger:** full name (surname and given name), passport number, passport type, passport expiry, gender, date of birth, nationality, email, phone country code and number.
- **Visa:** visa type (visa-exempt is an option), visa number, issue date, expiry date and place of issue, if you have one.
- **Trip:** expected arrival date, arrival airport or border gate, flight number, departure country before Vietnam (the first point of departure if you transit), purpose of travel, mode of transport, next city or port after Vietnam, departure date from Vietnam.
- **Stay:** accommodation type, province or city, ward or commune, and address.

A 6-digit code goes to your email before the form is sent. You can then download a PDF of the declaration and keep the QR code.

## Already submitted with a mistake

1. Open **Lookup & Update Declaration** on the portal. Enter the PAI number and passport number; an OTP goes to the email you used.
2. **Three fields cannot be updated:** date of arrival, passport expiry date and nationality. For those, the portal tells you to make a new declaration.
3. Lost the PAI number? The portal can look a declaration up by passport number, email and arrival date.
4. Still wrong when you land? The U.S. Embassy notice says the form is available at the airport by QR code or on paper.

## Fake sites

Vietnam's embassy in India warned in May 2026 that "several fraudulent websites impersonating the official platform have been detected". The only portal is `prearrival.immigration.gov.vn`. Read the address from the right: it must end in **`.gov.vn`**. The government form has no payment step, so a page asking for card details is not it.
