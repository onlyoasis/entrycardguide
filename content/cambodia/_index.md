## Who has to file it

- **Must file:** all travellers, free, within seven days before arrival or on arrival.
- **Exemptions:** the government page we read lists none. We found no official text on citizens, residents, crew or airside transit, so check with immigration if you are in one of those groups.
- **By nationality:** there is no UK, Hong Kong or Chinese version of the e-Arrival. Every traveller uses the same free form.

Source: {{< source-link site="cambodia.government_visitor_scope" text="Cambodian government visitor information" >}}. Whether you also need a visa is a separate question.

## The fast facts

- **Official URL:** `arrival.gov.kh`.
- **Cost:** arrival declaration is free. Visa fees are separate when required.
- **What you need:** passport, arrival details, contact email.
- **What you get:** an e-Arrival confirmation or QR.
