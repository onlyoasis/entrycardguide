## You may not need this visa

Start with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs list, not the payment page.

{{< official-link site="azerbaijan.visa_free" >}}

It separates ordinary-passport arrangements from diplomatic or service passports and gives the permitted stay. It also contains time-limited schemes with start and end dates. Check the exact passport and travel date.

If the trip is visa-free, there is no ASAN e-Visa form to submit. Do not pay an intermediary to file one.

## Check whether ASAN accepts the passport

Not every passport that needs a visa can use the online system.

{{< official-link site="azerbaijan.eligible_countries" >}}

The live eligibility page also flags temporary visa-free routes for specified passports. A passport can therefore appear in the broad e-Visa list while a current temporary rule means the first entries should be visa-free.

## Official portal and fee

{{< official-link site="azerbaijan.evisa" >}}

The operator is the State Agency for Public Service and Social Innovations under the President of Azerbaijan. The standard charge shown on the information page is:

- **USD 20 state fee**;
- **USD 5 service fee**;
- **USD 25 total** before any card conversion.

{{< official-link site="azerbaijan.information" >}}

The charge is non-refundable, including after refusal. Visa, Mastercard and UnionPay are accepted, and the card may belong to another person.

## Timing, validity and stay

{{< official-link site="azerbaijan.conditions" >}}

- Standard service: apply at least **3 working days** before arrival.
- Urgent service: a separate **3-hour** route, including holidays and non-working days.
- Validity: **90 days**.
- Entry: **single entry**.
- Stay: up to **30 days** within the validity period.
- Travel document: valid for at least **3 months after the e-Visa expires**.

The arrival document must be the same one selected in the application. Dual nationals should decide which passport they will carry before starting.

## What you receive

The portal emails the decision. Download and print the A4 e-Visa, then carry it with the same travel document. If the email is missing, use the official status checker rather than paying for another application.
