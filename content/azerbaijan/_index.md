Azerbaijan's ASAN e-Visa is a paid, single-entry visa. It is not the first step for every traveller. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs keeps a separate visa-free list, including arrangements with different passport types, stay limits and effective dates.

{{< official-link site="azerbaijan.visa_free" >}}

If that list covers the passport and trip, there is no e-Visa form to file. Otherwise, check whether the passport appears on the ASAN eligibility page.

## Fast facts

- **Official application:** `evisa.gov.az`.
- **Operator:** State Agency for Public Service and Social Innovations under the President of Azerbaijan.
- **Standard fee:** USD 20 state fee plus USD 5 service fee.
- **Standard timing:** apply at least 3 working days before arrival.
- **Urgent timing:** 3 hours, including holidays and non-working days.
- **Visa:** single entry, valid for 90 days, with a stay up to 30 days.
- **Passport buffer:** valid at least 3 months after the e-Visa expires.
- **Output:** an A4 e-Visa to print and carry with the same travel document.
