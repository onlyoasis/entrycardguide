The public first step shows nationality, travel-document type and entry choice. Later form validation attributes, character limits and error text were not readable without progressing through a real application. This guide does not invent them.

## 1. Check visa-free status

{{< official-link site="azerbaijan.visa_free" >}}

Use the passport you will actually carry. Read the passport type, stay limit and any start or end date. A visa-free traveller has no e-Visa form to complete and should pay nothing for one.

## 2. Confirm e-Visa eligibility

{{< official-link site="azerbaijan.eligible_countries" >}}

If a visa is required, check that ASAN lists the passport. The same page flags temporary visa-free rules that can override the usual e-Visa route for a limited number of entries.

## 3. Select the travel document

Choose nationality and document type according to the passport you will present. Dual nationals must use the same document for application and travel. Persons with refugee status are directed to a diplomatic mission.

## 4. Check the validity buffer

The document must remain valid for at least 3 months after the e-Visa expires. Do not count 3 months from arrival; count from the end of the visa's validity.

## 5. Make the photo-page scan readable

ASAN makes the applicant responsible for the scanned photo page. Include the complete page with no glare over the number, name, photo or dates. An unreadable copy can lead to refusal.

## 6. Leave enough processing time

{{< official-link site="azerbaijan.conditions" >}}

Standard service needs at least 3 working days before arrival. The urgent route is processed in 3 hours, including non-working days, but it is a separate service choice.

## 7. Use an email you control

Acceptance, payment and decision notices are sent to the entered address. Check the spelling. If the result does not arrive, use the official status checker rather than opening a duplicate application.

## 8. Pay only on the government portal

{{< official-link site="azerbaijan.information" >}}

The standard published total is USD 25: USD 20 state fee plus USD 5 service fee. The portal accepts Visa, Mastercard and UnionPay. The card may belong to another person. Payment is non-refundable.
