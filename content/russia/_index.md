Russia's unified e-visa is a **single-entry visa issued by the Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs**. It is not open to every passport: the ministry publishes a list of 64 states whose nationals can apply, and everyone else needs a regular visa from a Russian diplomatic mission or consular post.

{{< official-link site="russia.nationalities" >}}

Since 23 August 2025 the visa is valid for up to 120 days from issue, with a stay of no more than 30 days. Before that date it was 60 days and 16 days. Old guides still quote the shorter numbers.

## Fast facts

- **Official application:** `evisa.kdmid.ru`, filed from a personal account or the ministry's mobile app.
- **Agency:** Consular Department, Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Russian Federation.
- **Visa:** single entry, valid 120 days from issue, stay no more than 30 days.
- **Eligibility:** 64 states, published by the ministry.
- **Timing:** apply 4 to 86 days before entry; processing takes up to 4 calendar days.
- **Fee:** charged at the payment step, non-refundable; applicants under 6 pay nothing.
- **Border:** entry and exit must both use approved crossing points.
